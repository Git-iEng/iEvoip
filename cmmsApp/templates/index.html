How it works

From incoming call to the right outcome

iEvoip connects callers, staff and automation in one flow. A conversation can be answered by a team member, transferred to another person or team, or initially handled by an AI receptionist.

Your team stays connected through the browser softphone while call activity, history and supporting workflows remain visible in the phone-system experience.

  • Customer calls your business number
  • Rules or AI identify the request and route it
  • Your team answers through the browser softphone or configured destination
  • Call activity remains available for operational visibility
